Level

How to play
  • ON wakes it up. OFF stops it mid-sentence.
  • SPELL — it says a word, you type it and press ENTER. Two tries, ten words, then a score.
  • MYSTERY WORD — hangman. Six misses allowed.
  • LETTER — it calls out a letter, you find it.
  • SECRET CODE — type a word, hand the toy over, let someone decode it.
  • CLUE reveals letters, SAY IT repeats the word, REPEAT replays the last phrase, REPLAY restarts the round.
  • Your physical keyboard works too — letters, Enter, and Backspace to erase.
