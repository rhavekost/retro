Speak &Math

Level

How to play
  • ON wakes it up. OFF stops it mid-sentence.
  • SOLVE IT — it reads a problem, you type the answer and press ENTER. Two tries, ten problems, then a score.
  • GREATER / LESS — two numbers; press <, > or =.
  • NUMBER STUMPER — one operand is missing; supply it.
  • Levels A–D add subtraction, multiplication and division. Division always comes out even.
  • Your number keys work, plus Enter and Backspace.
