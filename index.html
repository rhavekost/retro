Retro

Browser recreations of the electronic toys I grew up with. No dependencies, no build step — every sound is synthesized in the browser, and every display is drawn from scratch.

  • 1978 Speak & Spell Ten words, two tries each, and it recites the correct spelling letter by letter when you miss. Plus Mystery Word, Letter and Secret Code. SVG 14-segment VFD · speech synthesis · 4 difficulty levels
  • 1965 See 'n Say Aim the arrow, pull the cord, and the farmer tells you what the cow says. Twelve animals on a spring-loaded dial. Web Audio synthesis · zero audio files · drag-to-pull cord
  • 1992 Talkboy Record thirty seconds onto the tape, then flick the switch and play yourself back at half speed. The reels really spool. Live mic recording · tape-speed pitch shift · synthesized deck noise
  • ? More on the way Simon, Merlin, Lite-Brite, the Big Trak — the shelf is not full yet.

Every animal call and every beep is generated at runtime from oscillators and filtered noise. Nothing here ships an audio file.
